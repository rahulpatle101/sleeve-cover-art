# Reflection

**Status:** COMPLETE · 2026-11-25

## What created the most delight?

Remix after the recipe audit. Every press now visibly moves (a new palette is guaranteed), and Harmonic draws a real figure (S-curves, chevrons, arches) instead of a grid. Keep rate rose from 14/25 to 47/49. Second: the dial feel after the dead-zone and axis-lock change; the operator called it "Feels right".

## Which craft pass changed the product most?

The **continuation Visual/Emotional audit**: judging 73 deterministic recipes as album covers, including a hold-out set. It produced the Harmonic drift normalisation, load-based stroke thinning, the complexity-capped Remix density, and the figure-only Harmonic Remix ratios. The first (19-minute) craft session fixed surface issues; the audit fixed the art.

## What did the extraction genuinely contribute?

- **Verified source learning (VERIFIED — EXTRACTION):** a light neutral shell with a near-black ink ramp; scarce functional hues; value-first parameter readouts (large value, small label); zero-radius fields with circles reserved for dials; an inset 2 px selection ring; breakpoints at 480/768/1200; marquee motion paused under reduced motion.
- **Our own judgment (PROPOSED — DIRECTION):** every SLEEVE colour value, the palettes, the system type stack, the dial behaviour (sensitivity, dead zone, axis lock), spacing, audio, motion and copy. The extraction captured no UI controls, so all control design and tactile behaviour is ours. Several extracted claims ("dark theme", role labels, generic rules) were contradicted and rejected.

## What was cut, and was that the right cut?

Nothing from Required v1 was cut. Deliberately omitted: volume control, undo/history and presets (operator constraints), and a bundled font (licensing). Right cuts: none of them blocks making and keeping a cover.

## Where did the four-hour plan slip?

Not in the direction expected. Build used ≈ 9 of 60 min and craft ≈ 67 of 120 min. The first craft session ended at 19 min, which led the operator to defer Gate C; the continuation produced the most valuable changes. Critique and ship then found **two S1 export defects** (a Chrome `toBlob` idle-time stall and focus loss from `disabled`) that every earlier automated pass missed, because none of them was strictly keyboard-only.

## What reusable principle moves to Day 004?

Audit generative output as **finished artifacts on a hold-out set**, not as valid geometry. Always run a **strict keyboard-only loop** (no DOM shortcuts) before calling an essential action done.

## One thing to improve if SLEEVE continues

Close the phone cause-and-effect gap (S2-1) with a compact in-view preview while tuning, so a Remix on a phone is seen, not just heard.
