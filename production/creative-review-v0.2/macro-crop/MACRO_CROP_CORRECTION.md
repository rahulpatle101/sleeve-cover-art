## The rule (`src/artwork/focus.ts`)

The rule is pure and deterministic. It works in trace units, so the preview and the 3000² export choose the same crop.

1. **Find structural events** on the key contour.
   - **Crossings:** self-intersections found with a segment grid, weighted by sin²θ. Crossings with |sin θ| < 0.3, meaning grazing or retraced overlaps, are ignored.
   - **Turns:** local curvature maxima tighter than 1/12 of the figure, weighted by sharpness.
   - **Channel relationship:** each event's weight is raised by up to +50 % × the registration amount where the Phase offset runs perpendicular to the contour. That is where ridge and groove visibly part.
2. **Score each event** by the Gaussian-weighted event mass around it, with σ = 0.18 of the visible window. Clusters beat isolated events. Ties go to the event nearer the figure centre.
3. **Place the winning event** at (0.50, 0.42) S. The scale is unchanged.
4. **Clamp the focus** 14 % inside the figure box, so the window always stays at least about 75 % over the figure.

If a figure has no events at all (a degenerate line), the previous placement is used. Across all 728 non-1:1 Harmonic Form × Rhythm × Phase combinations tested, this never happens.

The only other change is a three-line hook in `src/artwork/renderArtwork.ts`, active only when `set.macro && mode === 'harmonic'`.

### Pre-existing variance (not caused by this correction)

The Ink and Matter code paths were not modified. The differences above are run-to-run variance that already existed. I reproduced it on the unchanged path: Ink Newsprint Radial rendered differently in 1 of 24 renders, by one pixel. My working explanation is that the GPU returns values that differ by ±1:

- Ink: the ±1 lands exactly on the stochastic-screen threshold and flips one dot.
- Matter: the foot type's glyph edges move by ±1.

This is invisible at 100 %, but it means the "deterministic render" contract holds visually, not byte for byte. Making it byte-deterministic would mean forcing CPU canvases or quantising before the screen. That would alter the approved pixels, so **I have not changed it. It needs your decision.**
