# Mission — Day 003

**Status:** PROPOSED — DIRECTION

## Brief

Create **SLEEVE**, a visual synthesizer that makes generative graphic design feel as expressive and enjoyable as playing a musical instrument.

**Purpose:** help an independent musician or designer discover an album cover they genuinely want to keep.

**Design philosophy:** industrial precision meets artistic serendipity.

**Constraint:** one screen · one instrument · two mathematical modes · one beautiful output.

## Learning goals

- Transplant design-system principles without cloning a source.
- Turn deterministic mathematical parameters into legible, expressive controls.
- Make rotary interaction inclusive across pointer, keyboard, and assistive technology.
- Choreograph continuous visual feedback without sacrificing performance or reduced-motion support.
- Use original synthesized audio as optional feedback, never as a prerequisite.
- Export a production-useful high-resolution artifact from a client-only app.

## Definition of success

A first-time user can create and export a distinctive square cover in under three minutes, understands what every control changed, and wants to make another variation.

The daily challenge succeeds through depth of craft, not feature count.
