# Art direction — industrial precision, artistic serendipity

**Status:** PROPOSED — DIRECTION · not SkillUI-verified

SLEEVE should feel like a beautifully engineered instrument that happens to produce graphic design. It is quiet enough to invite focus, precise enough to earn trust, and surprising enough to reward play.

## Creative tension

- **Instrument, not dashboard:** controls are playable and causal, not administrative.
- **Album object, not demo canvas:** every state feels composed as a plausible cover.
- **Precise, not sterile:** mathematical order is offset by variation, texture, and human editorial judgment.
- **Distinct, not imitative:** reference principles may transfer after verification; brand identity does not.

## Proposed principles

1. **The artwork leads.** The square sleeve is the largest, highest-contrast object.
2. **Controls read as a family.** Shared anatomy, alignment, value language, and response.
3. **Functional color is scarce.** Accents explain mode, focus, state, or action.
4. **Motion reveals causality.** It helps the eye connect a gesture with a visual consequence.
5. **The result feels owned.** Artist and track are part of the design, and export feels like finishing an object.
6. **Restraint protects discovery.** Two engines and a small parameter set create depth through combination.

## One-screen composition

On desktop, prioritize a large square artwork stage alongside a compact instrument panel. On narrow screens, stage first and controls below. Keep primary actions—Remix and Export—unmistakable without making every surface a card.

The exact grid, colors, typography, and materials remain provisional until the SkillUI transfer ledger is approved.

## Avoid

- Generic glassmorphism, neon cyberpunk, or a dense DAW aesthetic.
- Decorative hardware details with no interactive meaning.
- Copying a reference’s exact grid, colors, labels, or product silhouette.
- Making the cover background compete with its title.
- Hiding values to make the interface look “minimal.”
- Adding controls merely to fill the panel.

## Craft sequence

Apply the six discipline documents in sprint order. When directions conflict, prioritize product comprehension and accessibility, then artwork quality, then novelty.
