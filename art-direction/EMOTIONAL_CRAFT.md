# Emotional craft

**Status:** PROPOSED — DIRECTION

## Desired arc

**Invitation → agency → discovery → recognition → ownership.**

The person should feel, “I understand how to play this,” then “I didn’t expect that,” and finally “this is mine.”

## Moments to design

### Invitation

Open with a credible cover, not an empty setup form. A small line—“Play your next album cover”—is enough orientation. Controls should look adjustable before hover.

### Agency

The first gesture must produce an obvious but controlled change. Stable labels and visible values build trust; the artwork should never appear to ignore input.

### Discovery

Parameter combinations create surprise inside a coherent aesthetic. Remix helps recover from a dull state without making all prior choices irrelevant.

### Recognition

Artist and track text turn abstract geometry into a plausible release. Let the person pause on the sleeve without UI noise taking over.

### Ownership

Export is a finishing ritual: the exact frame is rendered sharply, named intelligibly, and confirmed with dimensions. Do not watermark the result.

## Failure states

- A weak or overly dense result is recoverable with Remix or bounded control changes.
- Empty artist/track fields use quiet placeholders in preview but export should encourage meaningful names.
- Export failure preserves the current recipe and offers retry.
- Audio failure never makes the person feel their visual work is lost.

## Restraint

Avoid confetti, achievement language, streaks, gamification, or exaggerated claims of uniqueness. Satisfaction comes from authorship and craft.

## Pass checklist (15 minutes)

- [ ] The first screen invites action without a tutorial.
- [ ] The first adjustment creates a legible result.
- [ ] At least one combination genuinely surprises without breaking the art direction.
- [ ] A poor state is easy to recover from.
- [ ] The user’s identity text meaningfully changes the artifact.
- [ ] Export feels like completion, not a utility afterthought.
- [ ] Nothing overclaims originality, intelligence, or audio analysis.
