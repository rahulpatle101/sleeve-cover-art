# Sensory craft

**Status:** PROPOSED — DIRECTION

## Intent

Sound should confirm that SLEEVE is an instrument and deepen cause/effect without turning the project into a synthesizer product.

## Audio principles

- Off by default; begin only after explicit **Enable sound** action.
- Original synthesis only: Web Audio oscillators, envelopes, filters, and optional generated noise. No samples.
- Keep loudness conservative and provide immediate Mute plus a small volume control.
- Map sound and image from the same recipe so changes feel related, not random.
- Smooth frequency and gain changes to prevent clicks.
- Silence remains a complete, first-class experience.

## Proposed mapping

- Form and Rhythm define a two-oscillator ratio in a safe musical register.
- Phase applies subtle modulation or stereo relationship; avoid disorienting extremes.
- Density changes brightness/filter cutoff more than loudness.
- Mode selects timbral character: Harmonic cleaner/sine-led, Radial slightly richer but still gentle.
- Remix may produce one short resolved gesture after the new recipe settles.
- Export may use a quiet original confirmation tone if sound is enabled.

These are artistic mappings, not claims that the geometry represents real track audio.

## Safety and etiquette

- Never autoplay.
- Avoid sudden transients, sub-bass, harsh high frequencies, and escalating volume.
- Do not persist “sound on” across visits unless the product clearly communicates it; v1 should reset to off.
- The mute control stays visible whenever audio is active.
- If audio initialization fails, explain briefly and keep the visual workflow intact.

## Visual/tactile equivalents

Every audio cue must have a visible equivalent. The “tactile” feeling comes from visual detents, precise value changes, press states, and motion—not device vibration unless deliberately added later and made optional.

## Pass checklist (15 minutes)

- [ ] Sound starts only after explicit action.
- [ ] No external or copyrighted audio asset exists.
- [ ] Mute is instant and visible.
- [ ] Parameter changes are smooth and do not click.
- [ ] Maximum volume is comfortable.
- [ ] Visual experience loses no information when silent.
- [ ] Audio failure is nonblocking.
