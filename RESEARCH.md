# Research — 30-minute evidence plan

**Status:** PROPOSED PLAN · no extraction verified yet

## Research question

How can SLEEVE borrow the control clarity, hierarchy, restraint, and tactile confidence of a beautifully designed electronic instrument while becoming an original album-art tool?

## Primary extraction

**Proposed candidate:** a single canonical Teenage Engineering product page, chosen for control hierarchy and industrial interface language.

This candidate is not yet confirmed, captured, or extracted. Record the exact URL and access time in `research/extracted/PROVENANCE.md` during the sprint. Extract one page only. Do not combine multiple sites into a fictional unified design system.

## Secondary references (inspiration, not extraction evidence)

- Elektron — parameter feedback and expressive control density.
- Ryoji Ikeda — mathematical precision, repetition, contrast, audiovisual relationships.
- Peter Saville / Factory Records — album sleeves as cultural objects, typography, negative space.
- Patatap — immediacy and audiovisual cause/effect.

These names are research prompts from the project conversation, not verified audits. Any borrowed principle must be described in original language and translated for SLEEVE. Never copy artwork, brand assets, product copy, or a distinctive composition.

## Research outputs

1. `DESIGN_DNA_TRANSFER.md`: verified trait → evidence → SLEEVE adaptation → reason → risk.
2. A two-mode math decision in `GENERATIVE_MATHEMATICS.md`.
3. A short rejection list: traits that are too branded, irrelevant, inaccessible, or expensive.
4. Gate R result in `production/BUILD_LOG.md`.

## Evidence rules

- A trait is verified only if a reader can locate support in the preserved extraction or capture.
- Absence from the extraction is “unknown,” not evidence that the source lacks it.
- Proposed colors, typefaces, radii, motion, or sounds remain proposed until implemented and observed.
- Reference quality does not exempt SLEEVE from accessibility requirements.
- The extraction informs judgment; it does not override product scope.

## Five questions to answer

1. How does the source distinguish primary controls from secondary information?
2. What makes a control look adjustable before interaction?
3. How are labels, values, units, and states grouped?
4. Which spacing, contrast, and material cues can transfer without imitation?
5. Which traits should be rejected because they are brand-specific or harmful in software?

Stop at 30 minutes. Unanswered secondary questions go to the parking lot, not into the build phase.
